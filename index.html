About TraceNet

TraceNet is a smart phone-tracking platform designed to help users locate and manage their devices across Android and IOS.
It uses device identifiers and available network signals to help determine a device's location, provide location updates, send alerts, and display the device's last known position. TraceNet is designed with security and privacy in mind, giving users greater control and peace of mind when a phone is lost, misplaced, or needs to be located.

Functions Of TraceNet

1. Phone Location Tracking:Helps users view the current or last known location of a registered phone.

2. Find My Phone:Helps users locate a lost or misplaced phone.

3. Registration:Allows users to register and connect their Android or iOS phones to their TraceNet account.

4. Location History:Keeps a record of previous available locations of the phone.

5. Status:Shows whether the phone is online, offline, or was last detected at a particular time.

6. Battery Monitoring: Shows the phone's battery level when that information is available.

7. Location Alerts:Sends notifications when a registered phone is detected in a new location or becomes available again.

8. Multiple Device Tracking: Allows users to track more than one registered device from one account.

9. Offline/Lost Device Support: Stores the last known location and can update the device information when a new legitimate detection becomes available.

10. Privacy and Security: Protects users' tracking information and ensures that location sharing is authorized by the device owner.

In simple terms:TraceNet helps users register, track, locate, monitor, and recover their phones using available location and device-detection technologies.
